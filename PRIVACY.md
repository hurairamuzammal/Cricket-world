## 1. Overview & Commitment to Privacy

**World of Cricket** is an independent cricket statistics, live scoring, and machine learning analytics mobile application. We respect your privacy and believe that sports enthusiasts should enjoy matchday statistics without sacrificing their personal data.

**Key Commitment**: World of Cricket does **NOT** require user accounts, does **NOT** collect personal identities, and does **NOT** track user activity across apps or websites.

---

## 2. Information Handled by the Application

### A. Zero Personal Information Collected
- We do not collect, request, or store your name, phone number, email address, physical location, contacts, or financial details.
- The application operates out-of-the-box without registration or sign-in.

### B. On-Device Machine Learning Inference
- The **Win Wiz Probability Engine** and score projection models (TensorFlow Lite and ONNX Runtime) execute **100% locally on your device hardware**.
- Match scenarios, custom predictions, and simulation inputs are processed entirely in local memory and are never transmitted to external cloud servers.

### C. Factual Match Data & Public Information
- The application retrieves factual cricket match statistics, schedules, overs breakdowns, and public headlines solely to display them within the app.
- Cached match data is stored in the local application sandbox on your device and can be cleared at any time in system app settings.

---

## 3. Device Permissions

World of Cricket requests only the bare minimum permissions necessary to function:
- **INTERNET (`android.permission.INTERNET`)**: Required strictly to fetch live scores, match summaries, and sports news headlines.
- **ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE**: Required to determine offline/online status to switch smoothly to offline cached data.

The app does **not** request access to:
- Precise or approximate GPS location.
- Camera or microphone.
- Device contacts or call logs.
- External storage / photos.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services & Analytics

- **No Third-Party Advertising**: World of Cricket contains zero commercial advertisement SDKs, zero popups, and zero interstitial ads.
- **No Cross-App Tracking**: We do not integrate tracking frameworks (such as AppsFlyer, Adjust, Facebook SDK, or Google Analytics).
- **External News Articles**: When reading third-party news articles via external web links, the destination website's own privacy policy applies.
