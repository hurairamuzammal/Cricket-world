## ❓ Common Questions (FAQ)

### Q: Why are live scores occasionally delayed?
A: Real-time sports delivery depends on connectivity and upstream network latency. The app caches recent states locally so you can still read match summaries even during brief network drops.

### Q: Is my personal data safe?
A: Yes. World of Cricket does not require registration, does not collect personal data, and performs machine learning predictions 100% on-device using embedded TFLite and ONNX runtime engines.

### Q: How can I request new leagues or features?
A: Reach out via email or submit a GitHub issue!
